Expanded the Cinderlands entry to capture the historical state: at the moment of the seal, the wide zone around the Original's lair was visibly lifeless, studded with the newly-formed violet crystal formations into which surrounding corruption had crystallized. That image — a country of cinder, dotted with violet — is what the post-DoM continent remembered and named. Nature has slowly returned across the 2,300 years since, but the name persists as a pejorative for Spera.
